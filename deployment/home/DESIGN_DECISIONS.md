# Home design decisions

## Hero typography — 2026-10-21

- Direction: Editorial, selected from `/proto/hero-type/`.
- Typeface: the game's Audiowide title face, preserving the visual connection to Terraforming Contractor #1380.
- “Learn it.” and “Shape it.” carry the full hero hierarchy; “Shape it.” uses the mustard accent.
- The course name replaces the descriptive lead as a compact metadata line.
- Eyebrow: "Contract #1380 · multi-planet procedural sandbox". This matches the project's definition and avoids implying galaxy or star-cluster simulation.
- The original three-line statement, both calls to action, and the course name are retained in a compact black footer bar.
- Rejected: Orbit was more spatially dependent, System felt more operational than invitational, and Signal competed too strongly with the planet.

## Two ways in — 2026-10-21

- Both entry cards use solid earth-toned surfaces with custom topographic linework and subtle mineral grain; no card gradients.
- Separate contour maps distinguish play from learning without introducing another visual language.

## Hero entrance — 2026-10-21

- The planet appears centered first, then moves into its right-hand resting position while the hero copy enters from largest type to smallest.
- Motion uses transform and opacity only; reduced-motion users receive the complete final composition without an entrance sequence.
- The Worldbuilding mark returns to the game's voxel shovel icon.

## Residents transition — 2026-10-21

- The smooth fades above and below the Residents section are replaced with sparse, irregular pixel fields that become less dense away from each seam.
- Every pixel is one of the two neighboring surface colors; changing pixel density creates the tonal transition without introducing a continuous gradient.

## Field crew — 2026-10-21

- A four-role operations roster now sits between the entry cards and the residents, so the page introduces the agents of change before showing the inhabitants affected by it.
- Miner, Gardener, Rigger and Builder portraits use the production game models and role colors rather than parallel marketing illustrations.
- Each portrait is staged as a miniature working scene—mineral tunnel, planted ground, antenna signal and cut/fill terrain—so the function is visible before the supporting copy is read.
