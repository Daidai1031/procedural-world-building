Every little patch of ground faces some direction — imagine a tiny arrow poking straight out of it. On flat ground that arrow points straight up. On a cliff wall, it points sideways instead. **Steepness** measures how far that arrow has tipped away from "straight up": 0 for flat ground, 1 for a vertical wall.

Toggle **Height only** and **Height + slope**: slope adds a rock colour wherever steepness passes **Rock threshold**, fading in the same way Step 03's snow line did. Raise the threshold and only steeper patches turn to rock; lower it and gentler slopes join in too.

Orbit under a cave mouth: its downward-facing ceiling reads as maximally steep by this rule — a deliberate simplification of "steep," not a bug.

The erosion simulation in Lesson 02 also tracks water and sediment, but neither reaches this shader yet — that comparison stays a design until a channel is actually uploaded.
