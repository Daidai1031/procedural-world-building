Every point on the ground has a height number, but a colour blend wants a value from 0 to 1 — like turning "3.6 metres" into "72% of the way up," the way a fuel gauge reads a percentage instead of exact litres. That is what the highlighted line below does.

Toggle **Flat colour** and **Height gradient**: flat is one colour everywhere; the gradient blends three colours by that percentage, using `mix(a, b, t)` — literally mixing two paint colours by how far along `t` sits.

A gradient blends smoothly, but the snow cap needs a *soft switch* instead. `step(edge, x)` flips instantly from 0 to 1, like a light switch. `smoothstep(low, high, x)` fades over a short band, like a dimmer. Drag **Snow line** and watch the white cap climb or retreat — the threshold moved, not the terrain.
