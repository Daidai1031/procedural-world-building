Two of the scene's three lights fill in surfaces without casting shadows. They
need only the two tags below.

`ambientLight intensity={0.4}` adds light from every direction. A surface receives
that contribution regardless of which way it faces. It helps keep the unlit side
of the box visible. Rooms get a similar effect from light bouncing off many
surfaces; this tag is a cheap approximation of that scattered light.

`hemisphereLight` varies its contribution with a surface's orientation. Its `args`
list contains the sky color, the ground color, and intensity 0.5. `SKY_BOUNCE` and
`GROUND_BOUNCE` are named colors read from the site's tokens. Here they send cool
light down from above and warm light up from below. Look at the sphere's underside
and compare it with the top.

Neither tag has a position or a shadow map. These lights provide broad illumination;
the next light gives the scene a clear direction and casts its shadows.
