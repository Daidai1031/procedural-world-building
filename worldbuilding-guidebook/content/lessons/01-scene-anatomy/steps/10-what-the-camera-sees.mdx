The opening `Canvas` tag below comes from `SceneHost.jsx`. It owns the one shared
camera used throughout the course. This is an excerpt of the opening tag; the
controls and scene sit inside it.

Inside `camera={{...}}`, the named properties describe the camera's initial setup.
`position: [6, 5, 8]` places it 6 units right, 5 up, and 8 forward. `fov: 50` sets a
vertical field of view of 50 degrees. Widening that angle fits more into the view
but exaggerates perspective; narrowing it crops the view like a longer lens. The
controls aim it at `[0, 0.75, 0]`, just above the floor. You will see that target next.

`near: 0.1` and `far: 200` set the distances at which the camera starts and stops
drawing surfaces. Geometry outside that range is clipped. These values do not
limit where you can move the camera; the controls do that.

`shadows` enables the rendering needed by the previous step. `resize` with
`debounce: 0` lets the canvas follow size changes immediately when you swap it with
the plan inset. The camera stays where you move it as you navigate the lesson.
