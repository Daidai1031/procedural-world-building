The ground has two parts, shown one after the other below. The first `mesh` is the
floor. `planeGeometry args={[60, 60]}` makes a plane 60 units wide and high in its
own coordinates. `rotation={[-Math.PI / 2, 0, 0]}` turns it a quarter turn around x
so it lies horizontally.

`receiveShadow` lets the floor catch shadows, and `shadowMaterial opacity={0.35}`
draws only those shadows at 35% opacity. The plane itself stays invisible.

`Grid` is an imported component from Drei, a library of helpers for React Three
Fiber. Its capital letter marks it as a component; the import at the top of the
file supplies its definition. `args={[10, 10]}` gives its starting size, while
`infiniteGrid` extends it beyond those initial dimensions. Its position is 0.01 units above the
floor to keep the two surfaces apart.

Read `cellSize` and `sectionSize` to find the line spacing: 0.5 and 2.5 units.
Thickness and named palette colors distinguish fine lines from section lines.
`fadeDistance={35}` and `fadeStrength={1}` control how the grid disappears into the
distance. The grid gives scale; the floor holds the shadows.
