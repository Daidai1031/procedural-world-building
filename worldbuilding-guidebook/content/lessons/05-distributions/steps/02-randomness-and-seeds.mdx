---
title: Randomness and seeds
goal: Reproduce a random layout and distinguish layout changes from terrain changes.
keywords:
  - seed
  - pseudo-random
  - reproducibility
  - scatter seed
  - terrain seed
---

A **seeded random generator** produces a repeatable sequence of values. The scattering system uses that sequence to choose positions. Change the seed and a new layout appears; restore it and the original layout returns, as long as the algorithm, the settings, and the order in which values are drawn also stay the same.

Keep the **terrain seed** and the **scatter seed** apart. Changing only the scatter seed moves the rocks while the hills stay where they are. Random values are drawn when the layout is rebuilt, not again on every animation frame, or the rocks would jitter.

More candidates do not have to mean a completely different layout. If position samples are always drawn in the same order, the first candidates stay put and new ones are added after them. That only holds if extra random draws, for rotation or scale, are taken consistently too.

A seed alone is not a saved scene. It reproduces a layout only together with the rest of the generator's inputs.

*The interactive scene and practice for this step are planned but not built yet.*
