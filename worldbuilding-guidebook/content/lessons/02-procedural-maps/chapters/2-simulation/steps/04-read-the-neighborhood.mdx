Each cell looks north, east, south, and west. The coordinate offsets below describe those four neighbors; diagonal cells are excluded. At the edge of the map, an offset that would leave the grid is skipped.

World size changes the span of ground sampled by the grid. With the same cell count, a larger world puts neighboring samples farther apart and rebuilds the simulation. Flood fill also visits neighbors, but usually follows a connected region with a queue or stack. This erosion model instead updates every cell on every timestep.
