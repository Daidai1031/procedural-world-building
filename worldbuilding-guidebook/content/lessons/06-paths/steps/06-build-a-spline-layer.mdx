There are two possible shelters. First reject routes that the resident cannot pass. Then compare the remaining routes by travel distance and local disturbance.

Change **Disturbance** near the shorter route. A longer, quieter route may become preferable. Blocking both routes makes the resident wait.

The lesson uses two fixed shelter candidates so the route decision stays visible. Full cave detection and resident migration remain planned game features.

For the assignment, show a generated route, its terrain projection, and a local terrain edit. Explain why the route passes or fails. Keep the seed and settings so the result can be repeated.

Under **Inspect**, change the seed or restore the example. Settings pause and restart travel so comparisons stay still.
