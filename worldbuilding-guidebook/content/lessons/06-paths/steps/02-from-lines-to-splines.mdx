Straight segments make a sharp corner. A **spline** rounds the turn using control points.

Move the middle point beside the rock. Compare the straight route with the curve. A rounded turn can cut closer to the rock than expected.

Use **Bend offset** and watch the highlighted contact section. Smoothness describes the shape. The resident still needs enough room to pass.
