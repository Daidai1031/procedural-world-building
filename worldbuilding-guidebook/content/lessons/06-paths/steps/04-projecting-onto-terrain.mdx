A route drawn on a map has x and z positions. The terrain supplies each point's height.

On this height-field demo, set y to the ground height plus a small display offset. Widely spaced samples can leave connecting segments inside a hill.

Change **Sample spacing** and inspect the red sections. In the game's spherical, excavated world, surface queries must also choose the correct surface. One height lookup cannot describe a cave.

Switch **Projection view** to Map to see the plan above the hills. Vertical guides connect it to the sampled ground route.
