A resident needs to reach another shelter. A **path** lists the places it passes through, in order.

Start at the cave entrance, pass the rock, then reach the shelter. Changing the order changes the journey, even when the points stay put.

Try **Waypoint order**. Follow the numbered points and arrows. The line describes a proposed route; we will check whether it is passable later.
