title: Sampling a curve
goal: Place evenly spaced markers by sampling distance along the curve.

A **curve parameter** picks a position along a curve, from 0 at the start to 1 at the end. Equal steps in that parameter do not always travel equal distances in space. On a strongly curved route, markers placed at equal parameter steps can bunch up in some places and spread apart in others.

Sampling by **arc length**, the distance travelled along the curve, keeps the spacing more even. Three.js offers `getSpacedPoints()` for approximately equal-distance samples, next to `getPoints()` for equal parameter steps. Its accuracy depends on how finely the curve's length is approximated.

Asking for 20 divisions returns 21 points, because both ends are included. The markers are samples of the curve; how many there are has nothing to do with how many control points define its shape.

Even spacing on this flat curve can change once the route is laid onto uneven ground, so a projected route may need resampling.
