A curve parameter runs from 0 to 1. Equal parameter steps can cover different distances, making a moving resident speed up and slow down.

Sampling by **distance along the curve** gives steadier movement. The tangent tells the resident which way to face.

Switch **Sampling** between Parameter and Distance. The dots mark equal time intervals. Press **Step forward** and compare metres per tick. Equal arc distances mean steady speed; straight gaps can still differ around tight turns.
