Dust cannot be made forever, or the screen would fill up and the computer would slow down. Every speck needs a birth and an end.

An **emitter** is the tap that makes new specks. Each speck then gets older until it reaches its **lifetime**, and then it disappears.

Picture a shop. If 4 people walk in every minute and each stays 5 minutes, about 20 people are inside at any moment. Dust works the same way: the number alive settles near **emission rate × lifetime**. Right after you start, the count climbs first, like a shop opening in the morning.

Every system also has a **capacity**, the most specks it will hold. It is the room's fire limit.

Change **Rate** and **Lifetime** and watch the level bar reach the red tick. Doubling either one doubles the crowd, but the dust looks different: a short life with a high rate is a fast fizz, and a long life with a low rate is a slow haze. Press **Burst** for a sudden puff.
