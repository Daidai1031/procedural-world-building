Wind is invisible. A tiny moving dot barely shows it, but a **trail** does. A trail remembers each speck's last few positions and draws a line through them, like the wake behind a boat. The dot shows where it is, the trail shows where it has been.

Keep the memory short. A fixed number of recent positions is enough, and it stops the memory from growing without limit.

You can also let the look carry information. Colour by **speed** to show fast and slow air. Fade by **age**, which is age divided by lifetime, so a speck is solid when born and gone when it dies.

These are only costumes, as in Step 1. They never change how the speck moves.

Switch **Look** between Dots, Puffs and Trails, then turn on the colour and fade options.

Try turning off **Clear trail on respawn**. When a speck is reused, a long stray line jumps across the scene to where it restarted. That is why recycled specks must forget their history.
