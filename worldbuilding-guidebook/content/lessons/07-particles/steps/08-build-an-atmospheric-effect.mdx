Now put the pieces together. Choose something with no fixed shape: dust, mist, rain, or spores. Then answer four questions. Together they are your effect's **recipe**.

1. **Where are specks born?** For dust, a small area above a dig site.
2. **How long do they live?** Long enough to drift, short enough to vanish.
3. **What moves them?** For example a gentle breeze with a small whirlpool.
4. **How are they drawn?** Trails help you study the flow; soft puffs may suit the final look.

Then decide what happens at the ground. A wind map does not stop specks from sinking into the terrain, so remove any speck that falls below the surface. Keep a capacity limit and reuse specks so the effect can run all day.

Dust is only a picture. In the game, a separate "disturbance" number tells the resident how noisy the work is, and the dust just shows it.

Start from a **Preset**, adjust it, and read your recipe on the card. An abstract effect is fine if its rules and intent are clear.
