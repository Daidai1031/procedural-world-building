title: Position changes over time
goal: Move particles using velocity and elapsed time.

**Velocity** describes both a direction and a speed. To move a particle, multiply its velocity by the simulation's **time step** and add that displacement to its current position.

Here velocity is measured in world units per second and the time step, `dt`, in seconds. A particle moving at 2 units per second travels 1 unit in half a second. Moving a fixed amount on every rendered frame would instead tie the particle's speed to the frame rate.

Fixed simulation steps make runs easier to compare. Multiplying by `dt` alone does not guarantee identical numerical results for every complex simulation.

*The interactive scene and practice for this step are planned but not built yet.*
