Now the specks need to move. **Velocity** is a speed plus a direction, such as "2 metres per second to the right".

Each update, a speck moves by **velocity × dt**. Here **dt** is the number of seconds that this update lasts. Move 2 m/s for half a second and you travel 1 metre.

Why not just move a fixed amount each frame? Because screens differ. A slow laptop draws 30 frames a second and a fast monitor draws 144. A speck told "take one step per frame" would crawl on one and race on the other. Using dt keeps the speed the same on both.

Two runners start together. The blue one uses dt; the pink one takes a fixed step per frame. Press **Play**, then switch **Screen speed** between 30 and 144 frames per second and race again. Only the blue runner finishes in 5 seconds both times.

Press **Step forward** to advance one update and count the footprints.
