Think of a skateboard. A push does not teleport you. It makes you **faster**, and then your new speed carries you along. A force works the same way: it changes the velocity, not the position.

This change in velocity per second is called **acceleration**. Gravity is a steady downward acceleration. A breeze can be a small sideways one.

Each update follows two moves in a fixed order. First the push changes the speed. Then the new speed moves the speck. Programmers call this order semi-implicit Euler, but you only need to remember: speed first, position second.

A dig throws rubble out of the pit. Try **Gravity** and **Sideways push**, and press **Burst** for more. The dots left behind show the path bending.

Now press **Forces off** mid-flight. The rubble does not stop. It keeps its current speed and flies in a straight line, because nothing has taken that speed away.
