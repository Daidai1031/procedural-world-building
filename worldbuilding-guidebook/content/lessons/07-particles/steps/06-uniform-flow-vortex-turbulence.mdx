Real wind is a mix. You can build a believable wind map from three simple layers and add them together.

- **Breeze** is the same arrow everywhere, a steady drift in one direction.
- **Whirlpool** (a vortex) makes arrows circle around a chosen centre, like water going down a drain. A dust devil is a small one.
- **Gusts** are small, smooth changes in strength and direction from noise, the same idea as the noise maps in Lesson 02. Here "turbulence" means this artistic wobble, not real fluid physics.

At every spot, the arrows from the layers add up. A breeze plus a whirlpool gives a swirl near the centre while everything still drifts one way. Arrows pointing in opposite directions can cancel and leave a calm patch.

Switch each layer on and off, and drag the whirlpool's blue centre. Watch the arrows redraw and the stream of leaves follow them.
