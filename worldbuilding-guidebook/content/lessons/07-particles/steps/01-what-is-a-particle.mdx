---
title: What is a particle?
goal: Separate a particle's state from the way it is drawn.
keywords:

---

A **particle** is a small record of **state**: position, velocity, age, lifetime, and anything else the system needs. How it looks is a separate, rendering choice. The same particle can be drawn as a dot, as a textured **sprite**, or as the moving tip of a trail.

A sprite is a small image used to show a particle, such as a soft dust mark. In Three.js, a sprite always turns to face the camera. A visible dot does not need that texture, and a smoke texture does not decide how the particle moves.

Switching from dots to sprites while the system is paused should leave every position and every age exactly where it was. Size and texture change the appearance; they are not movement rules. Sprites and trails are ways of drawing particle state, not separate definitions of it.
