Now there are forks, but every piece has the same length and thickness, as if the tree were built from one kind of brick. Real branches get smaller as they reach outward.

Call the trunk depth 0, a branch off the trunk depth 1, and a branch off that depth 2. This count is the **branch depth**. Each level can keep a share of the one before: keep 70% of the length and the lengths are 1, 0.7, 0.49. Thickness works the same way.

Do not mix up two counters. **Generation** is how many rounds of rewriting made the string. **Depth** is how many brackets deep a branch sits. One generation holds several depths. Going forward along the trunk adds no depth, entering `[` adds one, and `]` restores the old level.

Switch **Shape** between Equal and By depth to see the mapping. Try length first, then thickness, then both. Click a branch to read its depth, and turn on **Color by depth**. Turn the colour off again: is the shape still readable? Which settings look like a plant, and which look like something unfamiliar?
