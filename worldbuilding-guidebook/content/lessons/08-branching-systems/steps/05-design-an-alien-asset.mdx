A species shares common traits, but its individuals do not have to be identical. Choose the character first, then add a little variation.

Pick a **Design**: Cave Fan forks wide and bushy, Stone Coral is short and compact, Whisper Root is tall and sparse. They are form experiments, not species decided for the game, and all three come from the same generator.

Switch **Look** between Skeleton and Solid. Wrapping tubes around the lines makes a solid, but a flat skeleton stays flat. **Spatial spread** lets branches leave the flat plane.

**Variation** nudges every angle and length by a small random amount. The **seed** is the number of that set of nudges: the same settings and seed always make the same asset.

Lock the seed and change the angle to make a compact and an open version. Then change the seed. Which setting made the main difference, and did variation hide it?
