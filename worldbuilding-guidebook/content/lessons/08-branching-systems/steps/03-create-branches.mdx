So far the pen has followed one route. To draw a fork, it has to step away and then come back. Two new symbols make that possible.

| Symbol | The pen does this |
| --- | --- |
| `[` | Remember the current position and heading |
| `]` | Go back to the latest saved spot not yet returned to, and restore its position and heading |

Take `F[+F]F`: draw a piece of trunk, remember the spot, turn left and draw a side branch, then go back and carry on along the trunk's original heading.

**Returning draws no line and does not erase the side branch.** Brackets come in pairs, and a branch inside a branch returns first.

Press **Next Instruction** and watch the ring. It marks the saved position, and its tick shows the saved heading. The dashed line is the move back, not part of the drawing.

Then switch **Branching rule** to One side or Both sides. The rule rewrites every `F` into a fork, so side branches can fork again. Compare the two at the same angle: where does the extra side branch start, and where is the pen after it?
