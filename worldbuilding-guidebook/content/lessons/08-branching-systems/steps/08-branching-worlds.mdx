Until now, rules decided how a branch unfolds. Now let rules decide how a world changes.

Two small planets start exactly the same: one cave, three residents who like quiet, and four quiet pockets. A robot works on each. In world A it works far away. In world B you choose where. The chain you can watch is **work spot → noise → a resident picks a quiet pocket it can reach → where the residents end up**.

A resident that stays above the noise threshold for 3 seconds looks for a pocket that has room, is quiet itself, and can be walked to. If there is none, it stays put, and that is a valid result. Nothing is scripted, and the same start always repeats the same run.

Press **Play** with B's robot near the residents. Then use **Sync Reset** and try Middle and Far end. Read the log and the overlay: who moved, and who did not? Then set Quiet pockets to None.

Optional: in class, play one round of Microscope with classmates, and write down only what really happened.
