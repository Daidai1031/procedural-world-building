Press generate and the finished asset simply appears. Would it feel more alive if it unfolded?

Here the whole structure is built first, and time only decides how much of it you see. Nothing is regrown and nothing is random, so dragging the timeline back and forth always shows the same picture for the same time.

Compare two ways of showing it. In **Together**, every piece grows at once. In **From the base**, a piece starts when the piece it hangs from has finished, so the trunk comes first and the side branches follow. That needs to know what each piece is attached to. Branch depth alone is not enough, because pieces within one level also follow one another.

This is a growth animation: it reveals a structure that already exists. It does not simulate food, injury or obstacles.

Pause at 0.5 in each mode. What has appeared? At the end, do both give the same asset?
