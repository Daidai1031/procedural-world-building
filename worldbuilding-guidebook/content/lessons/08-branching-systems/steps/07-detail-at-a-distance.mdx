Up close you can see a creature's small twigs. From far away you mostly see its outline. Does something far off need every detail drawn?

**LOD** (level of detail) keeps several versions of one asset. LOD 0 has every branch and 8 sides on each tube. LOD 1 drops the finest branches and uses 4 sides. LOD 2 keeps the trunk and the first side branches with 3 sides. All three are cut from one seeded asset, so nothing is regenerated and the root and main outline stay put. It is not growth, age or a different individual.

The table counts the triangles each level really draws. Fewer triangles means less geometry, but materials and shadows cost time too, so it is not a frame rate.

Compare the three side by side, then switch **View** to By distance and zoom out. A sudden change of shape is called **popping**. Set the switch distance very close to see it, then move it out until it is hard to notice. Which outlines did you keep?
