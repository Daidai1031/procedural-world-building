So far one formula described the whole world. **Constructive solid geometry** (CSG) builds a volume from two simpler ones instead. Here the ground is field `a` and a sphere is field `b`. Every sample gets a value from both, and one small rule combines them.

Because negative means solid, a smaller value is more inside. That keeps the rules short. **Union** keeps the smaller value, so a sample is solid if either field says so. **Intersection** keeps the larger value, so it is solid only when both agree. **Subtraction** flips `b` first, so what was inside the sphere becomes air.

Switch the operation in the control panel. The same two fields give a boulder on the ground, a hollow in it, and a lens of ground trapped inside the sphere. The highlighted line is the whole union rule. Subtraction is one small step longer, and the caves in Step 03 used it too, with the chamber already flipped.
