Chunks pay off most when something changes. Move the dent and only the samples inside its ball change. A chunk that holds none of them, border included, is exactly what it was, so its mesh can be kept.

Switch to Rebuild only what changed and drag the dent. The dark boxes are built again and the pale ones are not: the highlighted line looks a chunk up in memory before making it. The terrain comes out the same either way. Only the work differs. With 32 samples in 4 × 4 × 4 chunks, a dent reaches 13 of the 64 chunks on average, and rebuilding just those took about a quarter of the time.

A dent near a seam reaches both chunks, because both hold the border samples. Smaller chunks mean fewer samples to rebuild for each edit, but more borders to keep in step.
