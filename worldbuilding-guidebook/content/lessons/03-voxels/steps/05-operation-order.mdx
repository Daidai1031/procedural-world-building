CSG is not one operation but a sequence. The result of each operation becomes the input `a` of the next, so the order you write them in is part of the recipe.

Here the same three fields, the ground, a spherical room, and a rock, are combined in two orders. When you **carve the room first and add the rock second**, the rock arrives last and can push back into the hollow, refilling part of it. When you **add the rock first and carve the room second**, the room is carved last, so nothing can refill it, and it takes a bite out of the rock as well.

Switch between the two and watch the hollow. No field changed, only which operation ran first. In the highlighted lines, `withRoom` is the ground with the room already removed, and it becomes the `a` of the union that follows.

Where two edits overlap, the later one wins.
