Terraforming Contractor #1380 · S1 Resident Designs

Ares-9 Residents

Noise-averse, social rock residents that shelter in the ground. Nine design directions, each generated procedurally from a seed. Rotate to look around, switch behavior states, and reseed to see individual variation.

    Scores are a first pass for comparing directions, not conclusions. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click a resident to focus. Scale assumes a Miner about 0.15 game units across and residents about 0.06–0.13 tall; neither is settled in the game.
