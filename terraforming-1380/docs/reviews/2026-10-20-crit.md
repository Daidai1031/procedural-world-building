## Subsequent user decisions

These are design decisions from the conversation, not claims that the crit prescribed a specific feature.

- **Accepted:** procedural sandbox allowing free modification of different planets.
- **Accepted:** starting conditions → player/robot work → environmental change → resident response → different planetary outcomes.
- **Accepted:** residents “vote with their feet”; movement is feedback, not a mandatory good/bad score.
- **Accepted:** first residents are rock-like cave dwellers that prefer quiet; core preferences are snug cavities, low vibration and nearby companions.
- **Accepted:** discovered planets should remain visible in a 3D system overview with their changing shapes and concise hover information.
- **Accepted:** weaken detailed financial presentation; accounting may stay internal.
- **Open:** exact resident body, generation/tuning details, off-screen simulation, reload persistence and any replacement for bankruptcy.
- **Proposed:** separate antenna construction/discovery from Gardener work. Do not label this accepted or implemented.
