Water responds to the height of the whole surface: ground plus water. A cell checks its four neighbors and chooses the largest positive drop. If none is lower, it keeps its water for now. A low patch of ground can stop attracting flow once enough water has gathered there.

Flow rate controls how much water moves for a given drop, capped by the amount available. Try a lower rate, then a higher one. This model sends water toward one chosen neighbor per update; it is a simplified local rule, not a full fluid solver.
