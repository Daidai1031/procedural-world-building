Everything behind this card is one scene: four shapes, a floor, three lights, and a
camera looking at all of it. A scene is not a picture. It is a list of things and
where they sit in space, and the picture is what you get when a camera looks at that
list.

Those things fall into four groups, and the rest of this lesson takes one group at a
time. The shapes are **entities** — the objects that occupy space. The **lights**
decide how much of each surface you can actually see. The **camera** decides which
part of the scene reaches your screen. And the floor and grid make up the **world**,
the ground everything else stands on.

This same scene stays on screen for the whole lesson. Drag it to a view you like —
nothing will snap back as you move from step to step.
