The `OrbitControls` below sit inside the shared `Canvas`. They are another component
imported from Drei. `makeDefault` lets the app use these controls when it needs to
reset the view. `target={DEFAULT_TARGET}` points them at `[0, 0.75, 0]`, a constant
declared near the top of `SceneHost.jsx`.

Drag to orbit around that target, scroll to move closer or farther, and right-drag
to pan the target along with the view. `enableDamping` and `dampingFactor={0.08}`
make movement ease to a stop when you release the pointer.

The remaining values limit movement. `minDistance={3}` and `maxDistance={40}` keep
you between 3 and 40 units from the target. `maxPolarAngle={Math.PI / 2.05}` stops
the tilt just before you reach a horizontal view, keeping the camera above the
floor. These are control limits, separate from the camera's clipping distances.

Try each movement while watching the shapes and their shadows. The geometry,
materials, floor, and lights stay in place; you are changing where you look from.
That is why a new view can reveal a surface detail without changing the scene.
