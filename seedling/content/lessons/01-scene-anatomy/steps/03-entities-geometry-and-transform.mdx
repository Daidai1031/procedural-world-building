Select the orange box with a click or the control below. `Selectable` is an imported
app component that handles that click and enlarges the chosen mesh. Its `id="box"`
connects this shape to the picker. Everything inside it describes the actual object.

The `mesh` combines a shape and a surface. `boxGeometry` supplies the shape;
`args={[1.5, 1.5, 1.5]}` gives its width, height, and depth in world units. Curly
braces hold a JavaScript value, and square brackets group numbers into a list.
`position={[-2.5, 0.75, 0]}` lists x, y, and z: left or right, up or down, then back
or forward. The box sits 2.5 units left and 0.75 up, so its bottom rests on the floor.

`meshStandardMaterial` supplies the surface. `BOX_ORANGE` reads the site's color
token; roughness and metalness describe how that surface reflects light.
`envMapIntensity` controls the strength of the studio reflections. The next step
compares these properties. `castShadow` and `receiveShadow` let the mesh block light
and carry shadows. You will trace those settings when we reach the lights.
