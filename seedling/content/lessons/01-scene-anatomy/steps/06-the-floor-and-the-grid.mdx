The ground looks like one thing and is actually two, stacked a hundredth of a unit
apart so they do not fight over the same space.

The **grid** is a drawing, nothing more. Thin lines every 0.5 units, heavier lines
every 2.5, fading out entirely by 35 units away. It blocks no light and catches no
shadow. Its only job is to tell you how big things are and which way they are
facing — without it you would have no way to judge that the box is 1.5 units across.

The **floor** underneath it is a single large plane, 60 by 60 units. It is
completely invisible except where a shadow lands on it, and even there it only
darkens to 35%. It exists to catch shadows and nothing else.

Split this way, each part does one job well. A visible grid that affects no light,
and an invisible surface that holds the shadows.
