Click any shape in the scene, or use the control at the bottom of the screen. The
selected one grows very slightly so you can tell which it is.

Start with the orange box. It is three separate decisions stacked together. Its
**geometry** is the shape itself — a box, 1.5 units on every side. Its **material**
is what the surface is made of, which the next three steps take apart. Its
**position** is where it sits: 2.5 units left of the centre, 0.75 up off the floor,
and level with the middle front to back.

Change any one of the three and you have a different object. Keep all three and you
have the same box, every time, no matter what else happens in the scene. Shape, plus
surface, plus place — that is all an entity ever is.
