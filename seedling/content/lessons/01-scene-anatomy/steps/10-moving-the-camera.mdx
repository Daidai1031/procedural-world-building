Every way you have moved this scene so far comes from one piece: orbit controls.
Drag to swing around the target. Scroll to move closer or further. Right-drag to
slide the target itself, taking the whole view with it. Let go mid-drag and the
motion eases to a stop rather than stopping dead — a small thing that makes the
scene feel like an object rather than a diagram.

The movement is fenced in on purpose. You cannot get closer than 3 units or further
than 40, so the scene can never shrink to a dot or swallow the camera. And the tilt
stops just short of level with the floor, so you cannot orbit underneath the ground
and find yourself looking up through it at shapes lit from the wrong side.

That is the whole anatomy: entities, materials, a world, three lights, and a camera.
Every scene in this course is built from exactly these parts.
